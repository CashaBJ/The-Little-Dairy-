Back on the land

November 2026 · Casha Bowles-Jones

It's been quiet here for a while.

This past year I stepped back to look after myself. The land kept going, as it always does, and it reminded me of something I'd been slow to learn: growth needs rest as much as it needs work.

For ten years I've been regenerating this land with holistic and organic principles. I've watched the soil darken, the hedges fill and the birds return. The land's story speaks for itself.

What I'm learning now, through the Roots to Regeneration programme, is that changing systems starts with changing ourselves. So from here I'll share both: the land, and the inner work that goes with it.

This blog is where I'll write it down. Some posts will be about soil and grazing and the herd. Some will be about what's going on underneath. Most, I suspect, will be about the places where the two meet.

Thank you for being here.

Casha
