Why this beef is special

Raised with care from start to finish

This beef comes from a single dairy-bred steer, born here at Babbinswood Farm. Like every calf on our farm, he stayed with his mother and suckled from her until he weaned naturally. This is what cow-calf means, and it's the heart of how we farm.

He was 100% pasture fed: grass and anything else he chose to forage, with hay in the winter. Nothing else. He grew slowly, at his own pace, out in the fields with the herd.

He played an important part in the herd. As a steer, he can't go into the dairy herd like his female companions, but his job is just as important as he moves across the land following my careful holistic planned grazing. His hooves and his dung all help build soil on the land we've spent a decade regenerating.

Whilst only 18 months old, his beef is a little leaner than you might be used to, with a good, deep flavour and the beautiful marbling characteristic of his Jersey breeding. There's only so much of it, and when it's gone, it's gone.

Good to know

Packed, frozen and labelled

How it comes

Every pack is vacuum packed, frozen and labelled with the cut, weight, price, ear tag and dates. Frozen on 16 November 2026. Best before 16 May 2027 for steaks and joints, and 16 February 2027 for mince and diced beef.

Storing and defrosting

Keep frozen at −18°C or below. Defrost in the fridge and use within 24 hours. Please don't refreeze.

Fully traceable

Ear tag UK 317367 600084. Slaughtered and cut in the UK. Our mince is under 20% fat.
