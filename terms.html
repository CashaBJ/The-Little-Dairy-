4. Changes and cancellations by us

This is a working farm, and our cows and their calves come first. We may need to change or cancel a session at short notice, for example because of:

  • storms or other dangerous weather
  • a cow calving, or a cow or calf that is unwell or unsettled
  • a disease outbreak or movement restriction
  • illness or an emergency on the farm

If we cancel, you can choose a free move to another date or a full refund. We will refund within 14 days. We are not responsible for other costs, such as travel or accommodation.

We may also change what happens during a session, for example spending more time on the walk and less with the calves, if the animals need it.

5. Safety around the cows and calves

Cattle are large, strong animals, and mothers with young calves can be protective. Our cows are calm and used to people, but no animal is completely predictable. Each session starts with a short safety briefing.

During your visit, please:

  • follow Casha's instructions at all times, and move calmly and quietly
  • only touch, brush or approach the cows when Casha says it is safe
  • never walk between a cow and her calf, or stand behind a cow
  • don't feed the animals anything we haven't given you
  • wash your hands with soap and water after touching the animals, and before eating or drinking (hand gel alone is not enough)
  • wear wellies or sturdy boots and clothes for the weather; it is a working farm on clay and can be muddy
  • leave dogs at home, as they upset the cows and calves
  • not smoke or vape on the farm

Animals can carry germs that make people ill. If you are pregnant, have a weakened immune system or have a health condition, please speak to your doctor before booking and tell us when you book.

6. Children

Children of all ages are welcome. Every child must stay with their parent or a responsible adult at all times, and that adult is responsible for them throughout the visit. Please make sure children understand the safety rules, and keep little ones within reach near the cows.

7. Behaviour

We recommend you have your own insurance for cancellation, accidents and belongings. The cancellation terms in section 3 apply whether or not you are insured.

9. Complaints

If something isn't right, please tell Casha during your visit so we can put it right there and then. If it is still not resolved, please email us within 28 days of your visit and we will reply within 14 days.

10. Photos
