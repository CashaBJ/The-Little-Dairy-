Privacy and cookies

Last updated 21 November 2026

This page explains what information this website collects, why, and the choices you have. The website is run by The Little Dairy Ltd, Babbinswood Farm, Oswestry SY11 4PF. If you have any questions, email casha@thelittledairy.co.uk or call 07890 063993.

Cookies

Cookies are small files saved on your device by a website. We use cookies for Google Analytics, and only if you click Accept on our cookie banner. If you comment on the blog, our comment service may also set a cookie to keep you logged in (see below). If you click Decline, or don't choose, no analytics cookies are set.

We also save a small note in your browser of whether you accepted or declined, so we don't ask you on every page. It isn't used to track you.

What Google Analytics tells us

If you accept, Google Analytics gives us totals such as how many people visited, which pages they read, roughly where they were (town or country), what kind of device they used, and how they found us, for example from Instagram or a search. We can't see who you are.

Google provides this service and processes the information for us, and it may be handled on servers outside the UK. We have turned off Google's advertising features and its "Google signals" cross-device tracking. We keep analytics information for 14 months. You can read more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page. If you decline after accepting, we remove the Google Analytics cookies from this site.

Comments on the blog

Comments on our blog are provided by Remarkbox, which shows no adverts and doesn't track you. To comment, you give an email address, and Remarkbox sends you a link to confirm your comment. You can add a name if you'd like one shown with your comment. If you confirm, Remarkbox may set a cookie so you stay logged in to comment again; this only happens because you chose to comment. Read Remarkbox's privacy policy. If you'd like a comment removed, get in touch.

Fonts

This website uses Google Fonts to show its lettering. To load them, your browser connects to Google's servers, which can see your device's IP address. Google Fonts doesn't set cookies.

If you contact us or order from us

If you text, call or email us, for example to reserve beef, we use your name, contact details and order details to reply to you and arrange your order. If you choose courier delivery, we share your name, address and phone number with the courier. We keep order records for as long as we need to for our accounts, and we never sell your information.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Just get in touch using the details above. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
